# Showcase Prompts & Demo Commands

Use these sample commands in the **AI Voice Assistant** or **Live Market Rates** panel when presenting BoliLedger AI.

## 1. Hindi & Hinglish Voice Commands

- **Credit Sale (Udhaar)**
  - `"Rajesh Sharma ke naam 90 rupaye ki 3 masala chai aur 3 samosa udhaar likho"`
  - `"सुनीता देवी के खाते में 250 रुपये का दूध और चीनी उधार लिख दो"`

- **Cash / UPI Sale**
  - `"Aaj 5 packet Parle-G aur 2 litre Amul doodh cash mein becha"`
  - `"10 cup masala chai 150 rupaye UPI mein bik gayi"`

- **Payment Received (Jama)**
  - `"Manoj Kumar ne 500 rupaye UPI se udhaar wapas diye"`
  - `"विक्रम सिंह ने 200 रुपये नकद जमा कराए"`

- **Voice Inventory Restock**
  - `"15 kilo cheeni aur 20 packet Parle-G stock mein daalo, kul kharid 800 rupaye"`
  - `"Add 12 litre Amul milk to inventory"`

- **Operating Expense**
  - `"Aaj gas cylinder ka 850 rupaye kharcha likho"`
  - `"दुकान बिजली बिल 600 रुपये दिया"`

- **Natural Business Query**
  - `"Sunita Devi ka kitna udhaar baaki hai?"`
  - `"आज की कुल बिक्री कितनी हुई?"`
